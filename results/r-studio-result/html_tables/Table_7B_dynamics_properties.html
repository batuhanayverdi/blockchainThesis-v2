Note. Dispersion is the standard deviation of the sentence scores and volatility is the standard deviation of the differences between adjacent sentences. The implied adjacent-sentence correlation is recovered from the ratio of the two, since volatility equals dispersion multiplied by the square root of two times one minus that correlation. Values close to zero indicate that emotional language is distributed across sentences with little ordering, so volatility carries almost the same information as dispersion. Dispersion is therefore reported here but excluded from the models, and volatility is retained as the theoretically motivated measure.
